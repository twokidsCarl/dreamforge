Scientific illustration in David S. Only story light is luminous cyan-blue (#3FA9FF) with a soft glow. No text, no labels, no watermark. A single continuous membrane slab across the frame, slight perspective. Faint warm-white dotted motion trails (not blue) link the steps. Right third, separated by a soft gap: a CLOSED teal ion channel with a small synthetic azobenzene photoswitch tethered to its mouth, drawn as two linked benzene rings (two hexagons joined by an N=N bridge) in bright magenta, and a few more magenta two-ring molecules drifting down from above as if added from outside.
