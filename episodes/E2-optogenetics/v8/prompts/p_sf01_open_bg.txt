Art direction: premium cinematic science-documentary illustration — stylized 3D-illustration hybrid (Kurzgesagt-level clean shapes and depth, David Goodsell molecular color logic, HHMI BioInteractive cinematic lighting). Soft volumetric light and atmospheric haze, deep indigo-navy shadows (never pure black), warm cream highlights, matte painterly surfaces with very fine grain, subtle darker outlines on key silhouettes, clear foreground/midground/background depth. The only saturated 'story light' is luminous 470 nm cyan-blue (#3FA9FF). Absolutely no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark. Wide establishing shot inside living brain tissue: a dim forest of neurons in muted slate-violet and dusty rose, dendrites receding into haze. From the upper left, a single thin optical fiber tip emits a narrow cone of cyan-blue light that hits exactly one neuron in the center-right third; that neuron glows cyan from within, its neighbours stay dark. Large calm negative space in the left-center for a title. Elegant, quiet, awe.
