Scientific illustration in the style of David S. Goodsell's molecular watercolor paintings (flat pastel fills, thin dark outlines, cross-section view, painted texture). Wide horizontal composition of a cell membrane band across the middle. Left half: a slow multi-step relay — a light-sensing receptor protein (violet) catches a photon of blue light, passes a signal to a G-protein trimer (rose, orange, tan) that drifts along the inner leaflet, which in turn reaches and opens a distant ion channel (teal) at far left-center; the chain is clearly sequential with gentle motion trails. Right half: a different early approach — an ion channel (teal) with a small synthetic chemical photoswitch molecule (bright magenta, drawn as a tiny ball-and-stick tether) attached to its mouth, and a syringe-like droplet of added chemical floating above. The two halves are separated by a soft vertical gap of empty space. No text, no labels.
