Scientific illustration in the exact style of David S. Goodsell's molecular watercolor paintings (flat pastel fills, thin dark outlines, crowded but legible, cross-section view, painted texture). Cross-section through a neuron's cell membrane: lipid bilayer drawn as a band of pale yellow-ochre lipid heads with lighter tails; embedded in it, the light-gated ion channel channelrhodopsin-2 as a dimer of two seven-helix bundles in teal-green, each with a small orange retinal chromophore inside. A shaft of cyan-blue light enters from the top; the channel pore is open and small yellow sodium ions stream through from outside (top, crowded water with scattered ions and a few soluble proteins in muted blue and purple) to the cytoplasm below (crowded with pink-mauve cytoskeleton filaments and ribosome-like blobs). No text, no labels.
