Art direction: premium cinematic science-documentary illustration — stylized 3D-illustration hybrid (Kurzgesagt-level clean shapes and depth, David Goodsell molecular color logic, HHMI BioInteractive cinematic lighting). Soft volumetric light and atmospheric haze, deep indigo-navy shadows (never pure black), warm cream highlights, matte painterly surfaces with very fine grain, subtle darker outlines on key silhouettes, clear foreground/midground/background depth. The only saturated 'story light' is luminous 470 nm cyan-blue (#3FA9FF). Absolutely no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark. Wide establishing shot inside living brain tissue: a dim forest of many neurons of different shapes in muted slate-violet and dusty rose, dendrites receding into haze. From the upper right, a single thin optical fiber tip emits a broad soft cone of cyan-blue light over the right half of the frame. Inside the cone, only three neurons of one identical type (same pyramidal shape, spread apart) glow cyan from within with bright somas; all the other neurons inside and outside the cone, of other shapes, stay dark and untouched. Large calm dark negative space in the left 40% for a title.
