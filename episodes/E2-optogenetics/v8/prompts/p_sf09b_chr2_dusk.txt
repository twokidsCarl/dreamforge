Scientific illustration in David S. Goodsell's molecular painting style (flat fills, thin dark outlines, crowded but legible, cross-section, painted texture), but in a DUSK KEY to match a dark cinematic documentary: the extracellular water is deep indigo-teal (#13203F to #1C3350) with molecules in desaturated dusty blue and violet; the membrane is warm ochre-cream lipid heads (#E3C27D) with darker amber tails; the cytoplasm below is deep plum (#2A1735) crowded with muted mauve filaments and blobs. Only story light is luminous cyan-blue (#3FA9FF) with a soft glow. No text, no labels, no watermark. Center: the light-gated ion channel channelrhodopsin-2 as a dimer of two seven-helix bundles, mint-teal (#63C7A8) with darker teal outline, a small orange retinal chromophore (#FF8A3D) inside each half. A narrow shaft of cyan-blue light descends from top onto it; the pore between is open and glowing faintly cyan, and a stream of small yellow sodium ions (#FFD166) passes through it from top to bottom. Elsewhere in the membrane a few closed teal channels sit dark. Generous breathing room around the hero channel; camera slightly low so the membrane band reads as a slab with depth.
