Art direction: premium cinematic science-documentary illustration — stylized 3D-illustration hybrid (Kurzgesagt-level clean shapes and depth, David Goodsell molecular color logic, HHMI BioInteractive cinematic lighting). Soft volumetric light and atmospheric haze, deep indigo-navy shadows (never pure black), warm cream highlights, matte painterly surfaces with very fine grain, subtle darker outlines on key silhouettes, clear foreground/midground/background depth. The only saturated 'story light' is luminous 470 nm cyan-blue (#3FA9FF). Absolutely no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark. Visualizing a 1979 wish: a vast dense crowd of cortical neurons seen at shallow angle, mostly rendered dim grey-violet; scattered throughout, one specific type (pyramidal neurons, all the same triangular shape) is picked out in warm gold rim light, as if selected; others untouched. In the near foreground lower-left, the corner of an old 1979 science magazine page lies on a wooden desk under a warm desk lamp, its paper edge blending into the neuron field (page has only abstract grey lines, nothing readable).
