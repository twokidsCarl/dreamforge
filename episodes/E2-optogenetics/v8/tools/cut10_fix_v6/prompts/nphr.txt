Scientific illustration in David S. Goodsell molecular painting style (flat fills, thin dark outlines, crowded but legible, cross-section, painted texture), DUSK KEY matching a dark cinematic documentary: extracellular water deep indigo-teal (#13203F to #1C3350) with molecules in desaturated dusty blue and violet; the membrane is warm ochre-cream lipid heads (#E3C27D) with darker amber tails; cytoplasm below deep plum (#2A1735) crowded with muted mauve filaments and blobs. No text, no letters, no labels, no watermark. No ions drawn. Generous breathing room, camera slightly low, 3:2 landscape. Center: the light-driven chloride pump halorhodopsin (NpHR) as a single compact seven-helix membrane protein in warm saffron-amber (#E8A23A) with darker outline, clearly a PUMP: a closed bundle with no open pore, a small orange retinal inside. A soft shaft of 590 nm yellow-amber light comes diagonally from the upper RIGHT onto it. Other membrane proteins are grey-lavender (#8A86A8) and dusty rose, never amber. The pump sits slightly left of center so there is empty membrane on the right.
